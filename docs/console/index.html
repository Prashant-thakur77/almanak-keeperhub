Executions

Every transaction Almanak compiled and KeeperHub carried, newest first. Inspect asks KeeperHub for its verdict and reads the receipt events to show who acted, even when the relayer paid the gas.

Keeper on KeeperHub

A scheduled KeeperHub workflow generated from the strategy config: every tick it reads idle balance and, inside a bounded window, approves and deposits it into the same vault, with no Almanak process running. Almanak still decides entry, exit and anything larger.

Dry runs

Every KeeperHub simulation the backend asked for, including --simulate-only ticks. A refusal here is a transaction that never existed.

Failure modes, on purpose

What happens when things go wrong, recorded by the demo scripts. Refusals never reach the chain.

Measured, not asserted

From scripts/benchmark.py: refusals before broadcast, dry runs, real approvals landed and verified, a retry replayed instead of resent, and latency.
